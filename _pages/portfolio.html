--- layout: archive title: "Portfolio" permalink: /portfolio/ author_profile: true --- ## Research Portfolio ### Storybook Illustration Consistency My research investigates consistency in AI-generated storybook illustrations, with attention to character identity, narrative coherence, and visual continuity across generated images. Related publication: **Narratology Meets Text-to-Image: A Survey of Consistency in AI Generated Storybook Illustrations**. ### Multi-Character Identity Control I work on multimodal generative AI methods for controllable image generation and image editing, including systems that support coherent multi-character story illustration. Related publication: **CharCom: Composable Identity Control for Multi-Character Story Illustration**. ### Generative AI Applications My broader interests include text-to-image systems, controllable diffusion models, and AI-driven image editing tools that preserve contextual consistency while supporting user control.
