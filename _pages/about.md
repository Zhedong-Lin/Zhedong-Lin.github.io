      <p class="hero-lead">Hi, I’m <strong style="color: #fff;">Zhedong Lin (林哲栋)</strong>. I am currently preparing to begin my PhD studies at the <a href="https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en.html"><strong style="color: inherit;">University of Auckland</strong></a>, where I will continue my research under the supervision of <a href="https://www.liuailab.org/"><strong style="color: inherit;">Prof. Jiamou Liu</strong></a> and <a href="https://zhangxinyu-xyz.github.io/"><strong style="color: inherit;">Prof. Xinyu Zhang</strong></a>. I hold an MSc in <strong style="color: #fff;">Artificial Intelligence</strong> from the <a href="https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en.html"><strong style="color: inherit;">University of Auckland</strong></a> and received my BSc in <strong style="color: #fff;">Computer Science and Technology</strong> from <a href="https://www.swu.edu.cn/"><strong style="color: inherit;">Southwest University</strong></a> in 2025. My research interests include <strong style="color: #fff;">large language models (LLMs)</strong>, <strong style="color: #fff;">multimodal generative AI</strong>, and their applications in <strong style="color: #fff;">text-to-image synthesis</strong>, <strong style="color: #fff;">text-to-video generation</strong>, <strong style="color: #fff;">image and video editing</strong>, and <strong style="color: #fff;">controllable diffusion models</strong>. If you are interested in my research or would like to discuss potential collaborations, please feel free to contact me at <a href="mailto:zlin629@aucklanduni.ac.nz"><strong style="color: inherit;">zlin629@aucklanduni.ac.nz</strong></a>.</p>
